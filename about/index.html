About Lantern Arc

Accessibility engineering that starts with what people actually receive.

Lantern Arc develops software and research tools for testing whether digital and AI systems remain accessible in real use.

How we work

  • ClearExplain the failure without hiding behind jargon.
  • Evidence-drivenSupport important claims with reproducible evidence.
  • HumanFocus on access, information, outcomes, and user control.
  • DisciplinedState limitations instead of inflating what a test proves.

Our focus

Find the failure between “feature exists” and “user can actually use it.”

Lantern Arc focuses on whether critical information and communication remain usable for Deaf and Hard-of-Hearing people across AI, software, communication systems, devices, and real-world scenarios.

01

Test what the user receives

Do not stop at whether captions, alerts, or accessibility controls exist. Test the information and task outcome.

02

Measure what gets lost

Look for changed, missing, delayed, or unusable information across audio, text, visual, and haptic experiences.

03

Connect evidence to fixes

Accessibility engineering should fit into release, regression, remediation, and verification workflows.
