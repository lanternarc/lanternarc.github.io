DeafAccess by Lantern Arc

Test whether critical information stays accessible.

DeafAccess helps teams detect, prove, prioritize, fix, and retest communication failures affecting Deaf and Hard-of-Hearing users.

Explore the Labs Follow development

From finding to verified fix

  1. 01FindRun a real user scenario.
  2. 02ProveCapture evidence and impact.
  3. 03PrioritizeRoute actionable findings.
  4. 04FixConnect the finding to engineering work.
  5. 05RetestRepeat the same scenario.
  6. 06VerifyClose only when evidence passes.

Why it exists

A passing checklist can still miss the failure that matters.

DeafAccess is designed around the complete journey: can the user join, receive the information, understand it, act on it, recover if something fails, and finish the task?

01

Test the experience

Run scenarios across AI, software, media, devices, and real-world workflows instead of checking one isolated component.

02

Preserve critical information

Pay special attention to names, numbers, dates, amounts, codes, warnings, speaker attribution, and other details where a small error can change meaning.

03

Keep evidence attached

Connect the observed behavior, expected behavior, impact, environment, evidence, owner, and retest result.

Platform model

Platform + capability packs + workflow.

The Labs stay understandable by grouping specialized testing capabilities into a smaller number of customer-facing capability areas.

AI

AI & Communication

Speech, captions, agents, context, critical information, and regressions.

SE

Safety & Emergency

Alerts, emergency communication, real-time text, recovery, and public spaces.

DH

Devices & Hardware

Haptics, hearing devices, broadcast audio, vehicles, and vendor integrations.

ME

Media & Experience

Media, user experience, sign language, interpreting, DeafBlind access, and gaming.
