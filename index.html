DeafAccess capability network

Purpose-built Labs for real-world access.

Labs are specialized testing capabilities inside DeafAccess, organized around the ways information can be lost, changed, delayed, or made unusable.

30-capability roadmap: these Labs are specialized testing capabilities inside DeafAccess, not separate companies or standalone brands.

01

AI & Communication

Speech, captions, agents, context, critical information, and regression evidence.

DB

DeafBench

Benchmark speech, captioning, and AI behavior with reproducible evaluation.

CC

CaptionLab

Test caption quality, completeness, timing, and critical-information preservation.

CM

CommLab

Evaluate communication workflows across chat, meetings, calls, and messaging.

AG

AgentLab

Test voice and multimodal agents for accessible interaction and recovery.

CI

CriticalInfoLab

Measure whether names, numbers, codes, dates, amounts, and warnings survive intact.

CX

ContextLab

Test whether context needed to understand or act is preserved across modalities.

SC

ScenarioLab

Run realistic end-to-end accessibility scenarios instead of isolated checks.

NZ

NoiseLab

Stress communication systems under background noise and difficult input conditions.

CP

CaptionPersonalizationLab

Evaluate caption controls, readability preferences, and user-level customization.

MP

MultimodalParityLab

Measure what gets lost between audio, text, visual, and haptic modes.

RG

RegressionLab

Detect accessibility regressions between product, model, or configuration releases.

DT

DataTrustLab

Check evaluation data integrity, provenance, reproducibility, and evidence quality.

02

Safety & Emergency

Alerts, emergency communication, real-time text, recovery, and public-space access.

AL

AlertLab

Test whether important alerts have usable visual, text, or haptic equivalents.

EM

EmergencyLab

Evaluate urgent communication flows where delay or information loss can raise risk.

RT

RTTLab

Test real-time text continuity, timing, interoperability, and failure recovery.

RC

RecoveryLab

Measure whether a user can recover when captions, alerts, or communication features fail.

PS

PublicSpaceLab

Evaluate announcements, queues, kiosks, signage, and shared environments.

HR

HumanReviewLab

Support structured human review where automated evidence is not enough.

03

Devices & Hardware

Sound cues, haptics, hearing devices, broadcast audio, vehicles, and vendor integrations.

SQ

SoundCueLab

Identify sound-only information and verify meaningful non-audio alternatives.

HP

HapticLab

Evaluate vibration and tactile signals for clarity, timing, and equivalent meaning.

HD

HearingDeviceLab

Test connected-device workflows involving hearing aids and cochlear implant ecosystems.

AU

AuracastLab

Evaluate broadcast-audio discovery, connection, control, and accessible fallback paths.

AV

AutomotiveAccessibilityLab

Test vehicle alerts, infotainment, navigation, and connected-car communication for non-audio access.

VN

VendorLab

Evaluate third-party integrations and dependencies that affect accessible outcomes.

04

Media & Experience

User experience, media, sign language, interpreting, DeafBlind access, and gaming.

UX

UXLab

Test whether accessible controls and workflows remain understandable and operable end to end.

MD

MediaLab

Evaluate captions, transcripts, player controls, live media, and recorded content.

SL

SignLab

Evaluate sign-language experiences, presentation, controls, and modality handoffs.

IN

InterpreterLab

Test interpreter access workflows, handoffs, scheduling, visibility, and continuity.

DBL

DeafBlindLab

Evaluate combined visual, auditory, tactile, and text access requirements.

GA

GamingAccessibilityLab

Test gameplay communication, cues, captions, multiplayer access, and recovery.
